These Terms of Service govern the use of the INHS Sanjivani OPD WhatsApp Appointment Booking service.

1. About the Service

The service provides a WhatsApp-based mechanism for requesting and managing appointments for eligible Outpatient Department (OPD) services at INHS Sanjivani.

The service is intended to assist with appointment scheduling. It does not replace clinical consultation, medical advice, emergency medical services, or other hospital procedures.

2. Eligibility and Authorized Use

The service should be used by patients or persons authorized to make appointments on behalf of patients.

By using the service, the user agrees to provide information that is accurate to the best of their knowledge.

3. Appointment Information

Appointment availability is based on the OPD schedules and available appointment capacity configured in the appointment system.

An appointment is considered successfully created only when the service provides an appointment confirmation and appointment number.

Users should retain their appointment number for future reference and appointment-related requests.

4. Appointment Booking Rules

  • Appointments are subject to available OPD capacity.
  • Appointments are currently offered for the next available bookable appointment date according to the service configuration.
  • Different OPDs may be booked separately for the same appointment date.
  • The service may warn a user when selected OPD timings overlap. Such a warning does not necessarily prevent the user from proceeding.
  • A patient cannot maintain more than one active appointment for the same OPD on the same appointment date.
  • Cancelled appointments may become eligible for rebooking subject to available capacity and applicable rules.

5. Appointment Cancellation

Appointments may be cancelled through the supported appointment cancellation process.

Cancellation may cause the corresponding appointment capacity to become available for another patient.

An appointment that has already been cancelled, completed, or otherwise made inactive may not be cancellable again through the same process.

6. Accuracy of Information

Users are responsible for entering correct information during the appointment process.

In particular, users should carefully check the UHID, selected OPD, appointment date, and appointment details before completing a booking.

The WhatsApp appointment service stores the UHID entered by the user and does not independently verify the UHID against the hospital information system.

7. No Emergency Service

The WhatsApp appointment service is not an emergency medical service.

Users should not use the appointment service to report medical emergencies or situations requiring immediate medical attention.

Emergency situations should be handled through the appropriate emergency medical channels.
