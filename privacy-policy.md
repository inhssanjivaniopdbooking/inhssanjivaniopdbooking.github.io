# Privacy Policy

**INHS Sanjivani OPD WhatsApp Appointment Booking**

**Effective Date:** 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when patients use the INHS Sanjivani OPD WhatsApp Appointment Booking service.

## 1. About the Service

The INHS Sanjivani OPD WhatsApp Appointment Booking service allows patients to use WhatsApp to request and manage appointments for eligible Outpatient Department (OPD) services.

The service is intended to simplify appointment booking and provide appointment-related information through WhatsApp.

## 2. Information We Collect

When you use the service, we may collect and process the following information:

- Unique Hospital Identification (UHID) entered by the patient.
- WhatsApp telephone number associated with the incoming WhatsApp message.
- Selected OPD.
- Appointment date.
- Appointment information, including appointment number and OPD timing.
- Messages and information submitted by the patient during the appointment-booking conversation.
- Technical information required to process and maintain the appointment service.

The UHID entered through WhatsApp is stored as provided by the patient. The WhatsApp appointment service does not independently verify the UHID against the hospital information system.

## 3. How We Use the Information

The information collected through the service may be used to:

- Process OPD appointment requests.
- Create and manage appointment records.
- Prevent duplicate active appointments for the same patient and OPD on the same appointment date.
- Provide appointment confirmation and appointment-related information.
- Process appointment cancellation requests.
- Maintain operational records and audit information.
- Monitor and improve the reliability and security of the appointment service.

The service is designed specifically for appointment-related activities.

## 4. WhatsApp Information

The service operates through WhatsApp.

When a patient sends a message to the appointment service, the patient's WhatsApp telephone number and message information may be received and processed for the purpose of handling the appointment request.

WhatsApp also processes information in accordance with Meta's applicable terms, policies, and privacy practices.

## 5. Sharing of Information

Information collected through the appointment service is intended to be used for operating and administering the OPD appointment service.

Information may be processed by technical service providers required to operate the service, such as the WhatsApp platform and infrastructure providers supporting the appointment system.

Information will not be sold for advertising or marketing purposes.

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are intended to be used to protect appointment information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Access to appointment information should be limited to authorized personnel and systems that require such access for legitimate operational purposes.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Data Retention

Appointment and related information may be retained for as long as necessary for appointment administration, operational requirements, audit purposes, security, legal requirements, and other legitimate institutional requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable institutional requirements.

## 8. Patient Responsibilities

Patients should:

- Enter accurate information when using the service.
- Not provide another person's information without appropriate authorization.
- Not share confidential information unnecessarily through WhatsApp.
- Keep their WhatsApp account and device secure.

## 9. Children's Information

The appointment service is intended to be used by patients or their authorized representatives.

Where an appointment is being made for a child, the parent, guardian, or other authorized representative should provide the required information.

## 10. Data Deletion and Privacy Requests

Users who have questions about their personal information, wish to request correction of information, or wish to enquire about deletion of information may contact the hospital administration through the hospital's appropriate official communication channels.

Requests may be subject to applicable record-retention, legal, medical, audit, and operational requirements.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the appointment service, its data practices, or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this website.

## 12. Contact

For questions relating to the OPD WhatsApp Appointment Booking service or this Privacy Policy, users may contact INHS Sanjivani through its appropriate official administrative channels.
