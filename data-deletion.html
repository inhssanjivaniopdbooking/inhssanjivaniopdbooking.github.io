Data Deletion

INHS Sanjivani OPD WhatsApp Appointment Booking
Effective Date: 26 September 2026

This page explains how users can submit a request concerning deletion of information associated with the INHS Sanjivani OPD WhatsApp Appointment Booking service.

Important: Submission of a deletion request does not automatically mean that all information will be immediately deleted. Certain records may need to be retained where required for medical, administrative, audit, security, legal, or other legitimate institutional purposes.

1. How to Request Data Deletion

A user who wishes to enquire about deletion of information associated with the OPD WhatsApp Appointment Booking service should contact INHS Sanjivani through its appropriate official administrative communication channel.

The request should clearly indicate that it concerns information associated with the OPD WhatsApp Appointment Booking service.

2. Information That May Be Relevant

To help locate the appropriate appointment information, the user may be asked to provide information such as:

  • WhatsApp telephone number used with the service.
  • Appointment number, if available.
  • UHID entered during the appointment process.
  • Relevant appointment date.

Users should provide only information reasonably necessary to process the request.

3. Verification of Requests

Appropriate verification may be required before information is deleted or disclosed in response to a request.

Verification measures may be used to prevent unauthorized persons from requesting deletion or obtaining information belonging to another person.

4. Appointment Records

Cancellation of an appointment and deletion of personal information are separate processes.

Cancelling an appointment does not necessarily result in immediate deletion of all associated records.

5. Retention Requirements

Some information may need to be retained for legitimate institutional purposes, including appointment administration, audit requirements, security, legal obligations, medical record requirements, or other applicable requirements.

Where information is required to be retained, the applicable retention requirements may prevent immediate deletion of that information.

6. Processing of a Request

Requests will be reviewed through the appropriate administrative process.

Where deletion is permitted and appropriate, the relevant information may be deleted, anonymized, or otherwise handled according to the applicable institutional requirements.

7. WhatsApp and Meta

This page concerns information handled by the INHS Sanjivani OPD WhatsApp Appointment Booking service.

WhatsApp and Meta may separately process information in accordance with their own applicable terms, policies, and privacy practices.

8. Contact

For data deletion or privacy-related enquiries, users may contact INHS Sanjivani through its appropriate official administrative communication channels.

Users should not send sensitive medical information through this public webpage.
